## 🧩 About

**Rust Hack** is a comprehensive mod menu for Rust featuring ESP, aimbot, radar, loot tracker, no recoil, and config menu.

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Hacks
- ESP — see players, loot, and resources through walls
- Aimbot — auto-aim with customizable smoothness and FOV
- Radar — display nearby players and loot on a minimap
- No Recoil — eliminate weapon recoil
- Loot Tracker — track and filter loot spawns

### 🎨 Cosmetics & Unlocks
- Skin Unlocker — unlock all skins and items
